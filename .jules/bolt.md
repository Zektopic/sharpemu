## 2026-10-27 - CollectionsMarshal bypasses bounds checking overhead
**Context:** `src/SharpEmu.Core/Memory/VirtualMemory.cs`
**Learning:** In very hot paths executed millions of times per second (like guest address space page translation loops and insertions), typical `List<T>` indexer accesses introduce implicit MSIL bounds checking overhead on every iteration. Using `System.Runtime.InteropServices.CollectionsMarshal.AsSpan(list)` completely bypasses the indexer check and allows operations directly on the backing array memory. Combining this with the unsigned right shift (`>>> 1`) for binary search midpoint calculations generates optimal integer arithmetic.
**Action:** Always prefer `CollectionsMarshal.AsSpan` over `List<T>` indexing in guest-to-host MMU translation and execution hot loops where bounds are manually verified or strictly bounded.
