⚡ Bolt: Eliminate GC allocation in SnapshotRegions hot path

### 💡 What
Implemented a generational cache (via `SnapshotCache` record) in `PhysicalVirtualMemory.SnapshotRegions()` and `VirtualMemory.SnapshotRegions()`. This completely eliminates the per-call allocation of `VirtualMemoryRegion[]` arrays when the underlying memory mappings have not changed.

### 🎯 Subsystem & Bottleneck
- **Subsystem:** Guest MMU & Memory Subsystem (16 KB Pages / Unified Memory)
- **Target File(s):** `src/SharpEmu.Core/Memory/PhysicalVirtualMemory.cs`, `src/SharpEmu.Core/Memory/VirtualMemory.cs`
- **Bottleneck Addressed:** Repeated garbage collection pressure and CPU instruction overhead from allocating and iterating a new array of `VirtualMemoryRegion` on every call to `SnapshotRegions()`, which is deeply relied upon by hot path checks like `DirectExecutionBackend.ContainsAddress` via `IsGuestOwnedLazyCommitAddress`.
